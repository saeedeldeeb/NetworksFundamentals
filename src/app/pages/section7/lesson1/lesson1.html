Section 07

Network Performance

The bridge between theory and practice — how the fundamentals you know directly impact the speed, reliability, and scalability of the systems you build

Lesson 01

Introduction

You now understand how data moves across networks at every layer — from Ethernet frames to TCP segments to TLS handshakes. This section answers the question that comes next: so what? How does that knowledge change the way you write code?

From Theory to Practice

Every concept in the previous six sections has a direct performance implication. IP fragmentation wastes bandwidth. TCP's slow start adds latency. TLS handshakes cost RTTs. This section maps each mechanism to a concrete engineering decision you'll actually make.
